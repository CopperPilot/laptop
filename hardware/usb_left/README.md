# Left Dual USB-C PD & Data Daughter Board (`usb_left`)

## 1. Architecture & Block Summary (`usb_left_subboard`)

`usb_left` is a **4-layer 1.6 mm controlled-impedance FR-4 PCB** (matching the main board stackup with `0.1068 mm` outer prepreg over continuous `In1.Cu`, `In2.Cu`, and `B.Cu` `GND` reference planes). All components on `usb_left` are assembled on the top side (`F.Cu` only, `0` bottom-side components) so the CNC aluminum `bottom_shell` underneath `usb_left` is a continuous unperforated pan with no through-cutouts.

### 1.1 Front-Left 20 V (45 W Hardware-Capped from 45–65 W Adapters, with 5 V–20 V Autonomous Dead-Battery VINDPM Startup) USB-C PD 3.0 Charging & Host Data Port (`J2`, `TYPE-C-31-M-12`, LCSC `C165948`)
- **Autonomous Hardware USB-PD 3.0 Sink Controller (`U1`, `CH224A`, LCSC `C42459160`):**
  - `U1` (`CH224A` in ESSOP-10, 4 V–30 V operating range) negotiates **20 V USB-PD 3.0 contracts** from 45 W–65 W (`20 V @ 2.25 A–3.25 A`) USB-C PD adapters autonomously in hardware with no processor or I2C controller required (automatically falling back to the highest offered PDO `15 V -> 12 V -> 9 V -> 5 V` per `CH224` Datasheet Section 5.2 when `<20 V` adapters are connected), feeding the main-board `BQ25798RQMR` 5 A 2S buck-boost NVDC battery charger (`3.6 V–24 V` operating input, `26 V` `VBUS_OVP`, with hardware `VINDPM` [`4.3 V` floor at `5 V`, `V_VBUS0 - 1.4 V` at `12 V–20 V`] and hardware `ILIM_HIZ = 2.25 A` / `45 W` input limit set by `R29 = 78.7 kOhm` / `R30 = 100 kOhm`) over the 1.0 mm 5 A-rated harness `J5`.
  - Configured for **20 V PD request** using single-resistor `CFG1` strapping per WCH `CH224` Datasheet Section 5.2.1:
    - Pin 9 (`CFG1`, net `/PD_CFG1`) is strapped to `GND` via `R11 = 120 kOhm` 1% 0402 (`0402WGF1203TCE`, LCSC `C25750`) to request `20 V`.
    - Pins 2 (`CFG2_SCL`) and 3 (`CFG3_SDA`) are left open (`NC`, biased solely by `U1`'s internal pull-ups to its own internal `VDD33` rail), completely eliminating any external rail connection to `CFG2` and preventing CMOS back-powering when `U1.1` (`VHV`) is at `0 V`.
  - Pin 1 (`VHV`, internal 4.0 V–30 V LDO input) and Pin 8 (`VBUS`, voltage sense input) are tied directly together on net `/PD_VHV`, which connects directly to unswitched receptacle rail `/J2_VBUS` via 0-ohm jumper `R3 = 0R` 0603 (`0603WAF0000T5E`, LCSC `C21189`) and is decoupled to `GND` by `C5 = 1 uF` 50 V X5R 0603 (`CL10A105KB8NNNC`, LCSC `C15849`) per the `CH224A` application circuit (eliminating series voltage drop on `VHV` during 5 V cold-socket attach).
  - Pins 4 and 5 (`DP`, `DM`) are left unconnected (`NC`) per `CH224` Datasheet Section 5.5 (`Use PD protocol only`) so `J2` USB 2.0 host data lines pass undisturbed to the CM5 RP1 controller.
  - Pin 10 (`PG`, open-drain active-low 20 V Power Good indicator on `/PD_PG_N`) is pulled up to `/PD_VDD_3V3` (`3.3 V`) via `R5 = 10 kOhm` (`0402WGF1002TCE`, LCSC `C25744`) and drives a non-inverting 2-stage `2N7002` open-drain cross-domain buffer (`Q3` + `Q6`, LCSC `C8545`: `Q3` Gate on `/PD_PG_N`, Source on `/GND`, Drain `/PD_PG_INV` pulled up to `/PD_VDD_3V3` via `R24 = 100 kOhm` `C25741` driving `Q6` Gate, `Q6` Source on `/GND`, `Q6` Drain `/PD_PG_ISO` routed via `R16 = 1 kOhm` `C11702` to `J3` pin 1 `/USBL_PD_PG_N` and pulled up exclusively to the receiving main-board `/+3V3` domain via `R75 = 10 kOhm`, with `R25` removed), delivering CMOS-compliant active-low `20 V PD Power Good` telemetry (`V_OL = 0.065 V << 0.80 V = V_IL(max)` when `20 V` PD contract is active, `V_OH = 3.30 V > 2.00 V = V_IH(min)` when `<20 V` fallback source is attached or `J2` is unattached, with `0.0 uA` cross-domain bleed into `/J1_VDD_3V3` or `/+5V_USBC` in all power states).
- **Cold-Socket `cSnkPwr` Compliance (`<= 4.7 uF`), 22 V Flat-Clamping Surge TVS (`D6`, `D7` `ESTVS2200DRVR`), & `/U3_FLT_N`-Enabled Common-Source Back-to-Back PFET Load Switch (`Q1`, `Q2` `AO4407A` + `Q4` `2N7002`):**
  - Unswitched capacitance on `/J2_VBUS` (`J2.A4B9`, `J2.B4A9`) is strictly limited to `C12 = 1 uF` 50 V 0603 (`C15849`) + `C13 = 100 nF` 50 V 0402 (`C307331`) = **`1.1 uF` (`<= 4.7 uF` and `<= 10 uF` USB Type-C `cSnkPwr` max)**, with precision flat-clamping 22 V surge TVS `D6` (`ESTVS2200DRVR`, LCSC `C42400188`, `V_RWM = 22 V`, `V_BR = 25.8 V`, `V_C = 28.6 V max < 30 V` at 70 A 8/20 us) and bleed resistor `R2 = 10 kOhm` 0603 (`C25804`).
  - Downstream bulk capacitance on `/VBUS_PD` (`C15 = 10 uF` 50 V X5R 0805 `GRM21BR61H106KE43L`, LCSC `C440198` + `C16 = 100 nF` 50 V 0402 `C307331` + `D7 = ESTVS2200DRVR` `C42400188` at `J5`, plus main-board charger input capacitors) is isolated behind adjacent back-to-back **common-source** P-MOSFETs `Q1` (`(4.85, 35.30, rot=90)`) and `Q2` (`(12.80, 35.30, rot=-90)`) (`2x AO4407A`, LCSC `C2841482`, `-30 V`, `-12 A`, `13 mOhm` SOP-8):
    - `Q1` Drain (pins 5..8) connects to the `/J2_VBUS` `F.Cu` polygon pour; `Q1` and `Q2` common Source (pins 1..3) face each other directly across a continuous `F.Cu` polygon pour and copper bars on `/PD_SW_MID`; and `Q2` Drain (pins 5..8) connects to the `/VBUS_PD` `F.Cu` polygon pour feeding `J5`.
    - **Autonomous 5 V–20 V Dead-Battery Startup & CC OVP Fault Shutdown (`Q4` `2N7002` + `R15 = 47 kOhm` from `/U3_FLT_N`):**
      - Provided `U3` (`TPD6S300RUKR`) detects no CC OVP fault (`U3.9 ~FLT` high-Z, pulled up to `/PD_VDD_3V3 = 3.30 V` via `R6 = 10 kOhm` and driving only the insulated gate of `Q5` `2N7002` so `V(/U3_FLT_N) = 3.30 V >> 2.50 V = V_GS(th,max)` of `Q4` with `0.0 uA` main-board loading), `R15 = 47 kOhm` 1% 0402 (`0402WGF4702TCE`, LCSC `C25792`) pulls `/PD_EN` (`Q4.1` Gate) to `3.30 V`, turning `Q4` (`2N7002`, LCSC `C8545`) and `Q1/Q2` ON autonomously across the full `5 V–20 V` USB-C input range.
      - During dead-battery startup on `<20 V` (`5 V, 9 V, 12 V, 15 V`) or `20 V` USB-C adapters, `/VBUS_PD` (`J5`) powers main-board buck-boost charger `U_CHG` (`BQ25798RQMR`, `3.6 V–24 V` operating input), whose hardware **Input Voltage Dynamic Power Management (`VINDPM`)** automatically measures unloaded `V_VBUS0` prior to converter start (`VINDPM = 4.3 V` at `5 V`, `8.3 V` at `9 V`, `V_VBUS0 - 1.4 V` at `12 V–20 V`, per TI `BQ25798` Datasheet Section 8.3.3.2) and throttles input current in hardware whenever a `<20 V` source droops toward `VINDPM`, preventing adapter collapse without requiring host software while `ILIM_HIZ = 2.25 A` caps `20 V` PD input power at `45 W`.
      - When the CM5 is running, `U_CHG` immediately detects VBUS attachment on `/VBUS_PD` (asserting `/CHG_INT_N` to `CM5 GPIO5` and measuring exact input voltage via its 16-bit `VBUS_ADC` over I2C) while `J3.1` (`/USBL_PD_PG_N`) reports hardware `20 V PD Power Good` status (`LOW = 20 V PD contract`, `HIGH = <20 V fallback source`).
    - **Zero-Divider Full-Swing Gate Drive, Fast `~45 us` Fault Turn-Off, & Diode-Isolated Miller Pre-Charge (`R13 = 10 kOhm`, `R14 = 10 kOhm`, `D9`, `C17`, `D10`, `R22`, `D11`):**
      - When `Q4` is OFF (`/PD_EN = 0 V`), `R13 = 10 kOhm` 0603 (`0603WAF1002T5E`, LCSC `C25804`) pulls `Q4` Drain `/PD_DRV_D` up to the common-source midpoint `/PD_SW_MID` while dedicated series gate resistor `R14 = 10 kOhm` 0603 (`0603WAF1002T5E`, LCSC `C25804`) between `/PD_DRV_D` and `/PD_SW_G` and 12 V 500 mW Zener diode `D9` (`BZT52C12`, LCSC `C173429`, Cathode on `/PD_SW_MID`, Anode on `/PD_SW_G`) hold `V_GS1 = V_GS2 = 0 V` regardless of polarity, and discharge `/PD_SW_G` across `R13 + R14 = 20 kOhm` during a `U3` CC OVP fault (`|V_GS| < 1.7 V` in `~45 us`, `tau_off = 20 kOhm * 4.24 nF = 84.8 us`).
      - Miller soft-start capacitor `C17 = 10 nF` 50 V X7R 0402 (`CL05B103KB5NNNC`, LCSC `C15195`) on `/VBUS_PD` (`C17.1`) connects at `/PD_MILLER` (`C17.2`) to series blocking diode `D10` (`1N4148WS`, LCSC `C2128`, Anode on `/PD_MILLER`, Cathode on `/PD_SW_G`) and diode-isolated pre-charge network `R22 = 1 kOhm` 1% 0402 (`0402WGF1001TCE`, LCSC `C11702`) + `D11` (`1N4148WS`, LCSC `C2128`, Anode on `/PD_PRECHG` from `/PD_DRV_D` via `R22 = 1 kOhm`, Cathode on `/PD_MILLER`).
      - While `Q4` is OFF during hot-plug attach, `R13` (`10 kOhm`) + `R22` (`1 kOhm`) + `D11` pre-charge `C17` to `V(/PD_SW_MID)` with fast time constant `tau = (10k + 1k) * 10nF = 0.11 ms` (`3*tau = 0.33 ms < 0.50 ms`) while `D10` stays reverse-biased so `/PD_SW_G` is never pulled low prematurely.
      - The instant `Q4` turns ON (`V(/PD_DRV_D) = 0 V`), `D11` (`Anode` at `0 V`) becomes **reverse-biased**, completely disconnecting the pre-charge path from `/PD_MILLER` and `/PD_SW_G`. Consequently, **zero DC divider current** flows into `/PD_SW_G`: at `5 V` input, `V(/PD_SW_G) = 0 V` and `|V_GS1| = |V_GS2| = 5.00 V` (`100%` of the 5 V supply, well above `|V_GS(th,max)| = 3.0 V` and rated at `-4.5 V` `R_DS(on)`), while at `20 V` `D9` clamps `|V_GS1| = |V_GS2| = 12.0 V` (`P_R14 = 6.4 mW << 100 mW`, `P_D9 = 9.6 mW << 500 mW`) and `100%` of `C17`'s Miller displacement current flows through `D10` and `R14 = 10 kOhm` to control `dV(/VBUS_PD)/dt`.
- **24 V-Tolerant VBUS-to-CC Short Overvoltage & IEC 61000-4-2 ESD Port Protector (`U3`, `TPD6S300RUKR`, LCSC `C2649810`):**
  - Inserted between `J2` and `U1` (`CH224A`) / `J3`.
  - Connector-side pins `C_CC1` (pin 4) and `C_CC2` (pin 5) connect to `J2_CC1` (`J2.A5`) and `J2_CC2` (`J2.B5`), with dead-battery 5.1 kOhm `Rd` pins `RPD_G1` (pin 7) and `RPD_G2` (pin 6) tied to `J2_CC1` and `J2_CC2`.
  - `J2` USB 2.0 High-Speed differential pair (`J2.A6`/`B6` and `J2.A7`/`B7`) connects directly to `/USB3-0-D_P` and `/USB3-0-D_N` with low-capacitance (`2.5 pF` typ) IEC 61000-4-2 shunt ESD clamping on `U3.20` (`D1`) and `U3.19` (`D2`), while `U3` `C_SBU1`/`C_SBU2`/`SBU1`/`SBU2` are left unused (`NC`), matching the `U5` topology on `J1`.
  - `VPWR` (pin 10) is decoupled by `C9 = 100 nF` 50 V (`C307331`), `VBIAS` (pin 3) by `C8 = 100 nF` 50 V (`C307331`), and open-drain `~{FLT}` (pin 9, net `/U3_FLT_N`) is pulled up to `/PD_VDD_3V3` via `R6 = 10 kOhm` (`C25744`), biases `/PD_EN` via `R15 = 47 kOhm` (`C25792`), and drives a non-inverting 2-stage `2N7002` open-drain cross-domain buffer (`Q5` + `Q7`, LCSC `C8545`: `Q5` Gate on `/U3_FLT_N`, Source on `/GND`, Drain `/U3_FLT_INV` pulled up to `/PD_VDD_3V3` via `R23 = 100 kOhm` `C25741` driving `Q7` Gate, `Q7` Source on `/GND`, `Q7` Drain `/U3_FLT_ISO` routed via `R17 = 1 kOhm` `C11702` to `J3` pin 2 `/USBL_U3_FLT_N` and pulled up exclusively to the receiving main-board `/+3V3` domain via `R76 = 10 kOhm`, with `R26` removed).
- **Transient-Protected J2-Dedicated 3.3 V LDO (`U4`, `XC6216B332MR-G`, LCSC `C2891269`) with `R12 = 470 Ohm` + `D8` (`BZT52C24`, LCSC `C173422`) Pre-Clamp:**
  - Powered exclusively from `/J2_VBUS` via blocking diode `D3` (`1N4148WS`, LCSC `C2128`, net `/LDO_D3_K`) and RC surge current-limiting resistor `R12 = 470 Ohm` 0603 (`0603WAF4700T5E`, LCSC `C23179`, `100 mW`, `75 V`, dropping `< 46 mV` at `0.091 mA` 5 V attach load while limiting 28 V fault current into `D8` to `<= 9.57 mA` so `P_R12 <= 43 mW` and `P_D8 <= 218 mW`) into `/LDO_VIN` (`U4.1 VIN` and `U4.4 CE`), which is clamped by 24 V 500 mW Zener diode `D8` (`BZT52C24`, LCSC `C173422`, `22.8 V–25.2 V`, zero Zener bleed at 20 V PD) and decoupled by `C6 = 1 uF` 50 V 0603 (`C15849`).

### 1.2 Rear-Left USB-C 5 V DFP & Host Data Port (`J1`, `TYPE-C-31-M-12`, LCSC `C165948`)
- **Dedicated USB Type-C Cold-Socket DFP Controller & Power Switch (`U2`, `TPS25810RVCR`, LCSC `C473913`):**
  - `U2.IN1` (pins 2, 3), `U2.IN2` (pin 4), `U2.AUX` (pin 5), and `U2.CHG` (pin 7) are powered from `/+5V_USBC` (supplied directly from the main-board 6 A `/+5V` synchronous buck rail `U_BUCK` `TPS56628DDAR` with `>3.0 A` continuous headroom, bypassing `U_VBUS` `SY6280AAC`) while `U2.CHG_HI` (pin 8) is tied to `GND`, advertising **1.5 A USB Type-C DFP current** (`180 uA Rp` on `CC1`/`CC2`, with `U2`'s integrated UL-recognized `37 mOhm` power switch and `1.55 A min / 1.70 A typ / 1.85 A max` overload limit acting as the sole current-limiting element for `J1`).
  - `U2.EN` (pin 6, net `/J1_EN`, `V_IH(min) = 1.2 V`, `V_IL(max) = 0.4 V`) is locally pulled down to `/GND` via `R8 = 100 kOhm` (`0402WGF1003TCE`, LCSC `C25741`) and routed via `R21 = 1 kOhm` 1% 0402 (`0402WGF1001TCE`, LCSC `C11702`) to `J3` pin 8 (`/USBL_J1_EN`), which has a matching `100 kOhm` pull-down to `GND` (`R8`) on the main board and is driven from CM5 `VBUS_EN` (`3.3 V`) via `R91 = 10 kOhm` (gated low during low-battery load-shedding by `Q5` `BSS138`). Consequently, `/J1_EN` and `/USBL_J1_EN` default strictly low (`0.000 V < 0.4 V = V_IL(max)`) without fighting or self-latching when `/+5V_USBC` is powered, while driving `CM5_VBUS_EN` high (`3.30 V`) produces `V(/USBL_J1_EN) = 3.30 V * (100k || 101k) / (10k + (100k || 101k)) = 2.752 V >> 1.50 V = V_IH(min)` (`SY6280AAC`) and `V(/J1_EN) = 2.725 V >> 1.20 V = V_IH(min)` (`TPS25810RVCR`).
  - **USB Type-C `cSrcPwr` Bulk Input Capacitance (`>= 120 uF` on `U2.IN`) & Fast Cold-Socket Active Discharge (`tVBUSOFF << 650 ms` on `U2.OUT`):** `/+5V_USBC` (`U2.IN`) is decoupled by **`C1 = 150 uF` 10 V 55 mOhm Polymer Tantalum (`T520C157M010ATE055`, LCSC `C600588`)** + `C19 = 10 uF` 50 V X5R 0805 (`GRM21BR61H106KE43L`, LCSC `C440198`) + `C2 = 100 nF` 50 V X7R 0402 (`C307331`) + `D5 = TSD05CDYFR` (`C20611048`, 5.5 V working TVS) = **`160.1 uF >= 120 uF` `cSrcPwr`**, while switched output `/J1_VBUS` (`U2.OUT` pins 14, 15, with `C18` removed) has only `C3 = 10 uF` 50 V X5R 0805 (`GRM21BR61H106KE43L`, LCSC `C440198`) + `C4 = 100 nF` 50 V 0402 (`C307331`) = **`10.1 uF`** + `D4 = TSD05CDYFR` (`C20611048`, 5.5 V working TVS) so `U2`'s internal `500 Ohm` typ (`400–600 Ohm`) active discharge resistor discharges `/J1_VBUS` below `vSafe0V` (`<= 0.8 V`) in `~9.3 ms` typ / `13.7 ms` worst-case (`< tW_DCHG = 39–106 ms` and `<< tVBUSOFF = 650 ms`).
  - Open-drain status outputs `~{FAULT}` (pin 1, net `/J1_FAULT_N`) and `~{UFP}` (pin 19, net `/J1_UFP_N`) are routed via `R19 = 1 kOhm` and `R20 = 1 kOhm` (`0402WGF1001TCE`, LCSC `C11702`) to `J3` pins 4 (`/USBL_J1_FAULT_N`) and 7 (`/USBL_J1_UFP_N`) and pulled up exclusively to the receiving main-board `/+3V3` domain via `R78 = 10 kOhm` and `R79 = 10 kOhm` (with `R9` and `R10` removed on `usb_left`, eliminating back-powering into `/J1_VDD_3V3` and `/+5V_USBC` via `U6` when `J1` is disabled; `V_OL <= 0.30 V << 0.80 V = V_IL(max)`, `V_OH = 3.30 V > 2.00 V = V_IH(min)`).
- **Dedicated J1 3.3 V LDO (`U6`, `XC6206P332MR-G`, LCSC `C5446`) & CC/Data Port Protector (`U5`, `TPD6S300RUKR`, LCSC `C2649810`):**
  - `U6` (`XC6206P332MR-G`, SOT-23-3, `1.8 V–6.0 V` input, `3.3 V / 200 mA` output) regulates `/+5V_USBC` to `/J1_VDD_3V3` (`3.3 V`, decoupled by `C14 = 1 uF` 50 V 0603 `C15849` and `C11 = 100 nF` 50 V 0402 `C307331`), powering `U5.10` (`VPWR`) strictly from the main-board-derived `/+5V_USBC` domain (`0 V` when `J1` is disabled, with all switched `/J1_VDD_3V3` pull-ups `R7`/`R8`/`R9`/`R10`/`R25`/`R26` removed or re-referenced to `/GND` so `/J1_VDD_3V3` cannot be back-powered from main-board `/+3V3` pull-ups).
  - `U5` protects `J1_CC1`/`J1_CC2` (`C_CC1`/`C_CC2` -> `U2_CC1`/`U2_CC2`) and `USB3-1-D_P`/`USB3-1-D_N` (`D1`/`D2` pins 20, 19), with open-drain `~{FLT}` (pin 9, `/U5_FLT_N`, with `R7` removed) routed via `R18 = 1 kOhm` (`0402WGF1001TCE`, LCSC `C11702`) to `J3` pin 3 (`/USBL_U5_FLT_N`) and pulled up exclusively to the receiving main-board `/+3V3` domain via `R77 = 10 kOhm`. Because `J1` is a dedicated DFP, `U5.6` (`RPD_G2`) and `U5.7` (`RPD_G1`) are tied to `GND`.
